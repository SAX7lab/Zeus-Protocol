2. The short version

Your blood test results never leave your device. Everything else is stored only if you create an account, lives on a server in Frankfurt, is never sold, never shared with advertisers, and is deleted completely when you delete your account.

3. Using the app without an account

The app works fully without signing up. In that case all data stays in your browser's local storage on your device. Nothing is transmitted anywhere, and no personal data is processed by the controller at all.

Clearing your browser data or deleting the app removes it permanently. Use the built-in backup export if you want a copy.

4. What is stored when you do create an account

Account data

Your email address and a hashed password. Passwords are hashed by Supabase and cannot be read by anyone, including the controller.

Application data

  • Routines, supplement schedules and tasks you create
  • Which items you complete each day, and streak counts
  • Calendar subscription addresses and display settings
  • Your display name and which sections of the app you enable

Health and activity data

Only if you connect a source yourself:

  • Apple Health — daily step count and exercise minutes, sent by a Shortcut you install and control. No other health category is read.
  • Whoop — sleep and recovery metrics, retrieved with the access you grant during the Whoop authorisation step.

Blood panels — stored on your device only

Laboratory reports you import are parsed in your browser and saved only in local storage. The PDF is never uploaded, and the extracted values are excluded from cloud synchronisation by default. An opt-in setting exists for the operator's own account; if you enable it, the values are stored alongside your other data and you can disable it again, which deletes them from the server.

7. What is not done

  • No advertising, and no data sold or shared with advertisers
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or third-party cookies
  • No profiling and no automated decision-making
  • No transfer to third parties beyond the processors listed above

8. How long data is kept

Until you delete it. Deleting your account removes every record immediately and irreversibly; deletion cascades across all tables. There are no separate backups from which data could be restored, so export first if you want a copy.

Accounts inactive for more than 24 months may be deleted after notice by email.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and object to processing. In practice:

  • Access and portability — the backup export in the app produces a complete JSON file of everything held about you
  • Erasure — Delete account, in the account panel
  • Withdrawal — disconnect Apple Health or Whoop at any time

You may also lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at.

10. Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. Access is enforced at the database level by row-level security, so one account cannot read another's records even if the application itself were flawed. Device tokens used by the Apple Health Shortcut are stored only as hashes and can be revoked individually.

No system is perfectly secure. In the event of a breach affecting your data, you will be informed without undue delay, as required by Article 34 GDPR.

11. Children

This application is not intended for anyone under 16, and accounts should not be created for them.

If this policy changes materially, you will be asked to review and accept the new version the next time you sign in. The version number and date above indicate the current text.
